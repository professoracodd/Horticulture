How to play

You and your rival share CeCe, a white fluffy Maltipoo, for one day: 7am to 10pm, squeezed into a few real minutes. Every good decision earns you points. When the clock runs out, the highest score wins.

Pick your difficulty

  • Easy — 5 minutes. CeCe is patient and her needs arrive one at a time.
  • Medium — 4 minutes. Needs overlap and there is a real race for points.
  • Hard — 3 minutes. Everything at once, and she loses patience fast.

Get to know CeCe

  • She loves soft fresh food, warmed in the microwave for 30 seconds. She won't eat kibble.
  • She likes her water fresh and cold.
  • She hates wet paws and wet grass. Rain and morning dew soak the yard, so use the covered patio. If her paws get wet, towel-dry them.
  • She hates being dirty. Keep her brushed.
  • When she has bad breath, give her a breath mint.
  • She adores her little stuffed sheep, running around the house, lying in her bed, and licking your face.

Read CeCe

Needs aren't shown on screen all the time. Watch CeCe: pacing by the door means a potty break is due, staring at her bowl means she's hungry, droopy ears and a low tail mean she's sad. Tap Check any time for exact numbers.

Timing is everything

  • Meeting a real need the way CeCe likes it earns full points. Doing it wrong or when it isn't needed earns little or costs points.
  • Responding to a critical need earns a rescue bonus.
  • Each action has a personal cooldown (shown in game time), and CeCe can only do one thing at a time. Beat your rival to the right action.
  • Accidents and long neglect cost both players points.

Points at a glance

  • Potty break +15 · Cuddle up to +14 · Feed +10 · Groom +10 · Play +10 · Water +8 · Mint +8 · Towel-dry +8 · Bedtime +8
  • Rescue bonus +4 to +6 · Personality discovery +5
  • Accident −15 each · Overfeed −5 · Kibble −3 · Neglect −2 per critical need per game hour

Hidden personality

CeCe also has moods and quirks that change from game to game. Interact enough and you'll uncover each one for a bonus.
