SaniDate® 5.0 - Safety Data Sheet (Abridged)

Hazard Identification

Signal Word: DANGER

Components: Hydrogen Peroxide (16-26%), Peroxyacetic Acid (4.2-6%), Acetic Acid (8-12%)

Causes severe skin burns and eye damage. Harmful if swallowed or inhaled. May intensify fire; oxidizer. pH: <1 (highly acidic)

Section 8: Exposure Controls / Personal Protection

Engineering Controls: Ensure adequate ventilation. Emergency eye wash stations and showers should be available.

Respiratory Protection:

  • Use NIOSH-approved air-purifying or supplied air respirator
  • Recommended: Organic vapor / acid gas cartridge respirator

Eye / Face Protection:

  • Chemical resistant goggles or face shield required
  • Required if splashes are expected to occur

Hand Protection:

  • Rubber, latex, neoprene, or other chemical resistant gloves
DO NOT use leather or cotton gloves. These materials absorb chemicals and can cause prolonged skin exposure.

Skin / Body Protection:

  • Wear non-combustible clothing and footwear (PVC, neoprene, nitrile, or natural rubber)
  • Chemical-resistant footwear (rubber boots)
