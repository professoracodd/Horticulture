SaniDate® 5.0 - Safety Data Sheet

Hazard Identification

Signal Word: DANGER

Components: Hydrogen Peroxide (16-26%), Peroxyacetic Acid (4.2-6%), Acetic Acid (8-12%)

Causes severe skin burns and eye damage. Harmful if swallowed or inhaled.

Personal Protection

Respiratory:

  • Organic vapor / acid gas cartridge respirator

Eye Protection:

  • Chemical resistant goggles required

Hand Protection:

  • Rubber, latex, neoprene, or nitrile gloves
DO NOT use leather or cotton gloves. These absorb chemicals.

Body/Feet:

  • PVC, neoprene, or rubber clothing
  • Chemical-resistant rubber boots
