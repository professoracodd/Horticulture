SaniDate® 5.0 - Safety Data Sheet

Hazard Identification

Signal Word: DANGER

Components: Hydrogen Peroxide (23%), Peroxyacetic Acid (5.3%)

Causes severe skin burns and eye damage. Harmful if swallowed or inhaled. May cause respiratory irritation.

Personal Protection (Section 8)

Respiratory Protection:

  • NIOSH-approved air-purifying or supplied air respirator
  • Organic vapor / acid gas cartridge recommended

Eye / Face Protection:

  • Chemical resistant goggles or face shield

Hand Protection:

  • Rubber, latex, neoprene, or other chemical resistant gloves
DO NOT use leather or cotton gloves. These materials absorb chemicals.

Skin / Body Protection:

  • Non-combustible clothing (PVC, neoprene, nitrile, or natural rubber)
  • Chemical-resistant footwear (rubber boots)
