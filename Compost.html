Build your pile

Click a material to add a 5-gallon bucket to your pile. Each one lists its own carbon-to-nitrogen ratio — how you combine them is up to you.

👋 First time here?

Use the + and − buttons to add ingredients from three categories: Greens, Browns, and High Nitrogen. Watch the stats update as you go — aim for roughly 40 buckets total (1 yd³) and a blended C:N near 30:1. Or click Try a Classic 30:1 Mix below to see a working recipe first. Then move through Water → Turn → Cure → Shake to discover what your pile actually produces.

The shake test

Shake a small sample of compost in a test tube with 15 mL of water. The color of the water tells you how finished your compost is. The microscope reveals what's actually alive in it.
